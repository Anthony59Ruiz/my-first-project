About

Code in the morning.
Pages at night.

I live in Los Angeles and spend my time doing two things most people keep separate: building software and writing screenplays. The tools I build are sharp and purposeful — custom websites, automation pipelines, AI applications that actually solve something. The scripts I write are character-first, structure-obsessed, and set in worlds that feel lived-in. Working in both disciplines has convinced me they run on the same fuel: a clear problem, a point of view, and the discipline to cut everything that doesn't serve the story.

I recently walked away from a full-time role to go independent — which means I'm now doing this work on my own terms, in my own city, with my own clients. I build with Claude Code and ship fast. I develop screenplays between projects and without apology. If you're looking for a developer who thinks like a writer — or you just need something built well, by someone who gives a damn — let's talk.
